An overpayment scam targets sellers, and also people offering jobs, rentals and services. The buyer "accidentally" pays too much, then asks you to send the difference back.

## How the scam works

Two versions are common:

- **The fake email.** The buyer sends an email that looks like it came from a payment service, saying the money has been received, or that they overpaid. The email is fake. Nothing arrives in your account, and you lose both the item and any refund you sent.
- **The bad check.** The buyer pays with a check for more than the price. Your bank may make the money look available for a while, but when the check bounces, you owe the bank the full amount, including the refund you sent.

## What to do

- Don't click links in a payment email. Log in to your payment app or bank yourself, and check that the money has fully cleared, not just appears as available.
- Don't send money back, and don't send the item, until it has cleared.
- Be careful with buyers who want to move the deal away from the selling site.
- Save the messages and emails as evidence.
- In the US, report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov). In Australia, report it to [Scamwatch](https://www.scamwatch.gov.au).
