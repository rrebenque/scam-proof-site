This scam is hard to spot because the email really does come from PayPal. Anyone with an account can send an invoice, and the scammer writes their own phone number into the note. PayPal says not to pay these invoices and not to call numbers in the note.

## What to do

- Don't pay the invoice, and don't call the number in it.
- Open the PayPal app, or type the website address yourself, and log in. Report the invoice from there.
- If you received a fake PayPal email that didn't come from PayPal, PayPal asks you to forward it to phishing@paypal.com.
- An invoice by itself doesn't take money from you. You owe nothing just because one arrived.

## Already called or let them in?

- Disconnect the computer from the internet.
- Call your bank on the number on your card, and change your PayPal and email passwords from another device.
- Go to [I've been scammed](/been-scammed/) for where to report.
