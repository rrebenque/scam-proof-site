Task scams are also called gamified job scams. They usually start with a text, WhatsApp or Telegram message you weren't expecting, offering quick money for simple online "tasks."

## Why it works

The first payout is real, but small. The US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) says scammers often pay $5 to $20 to earn your trust. After that, the app shows your earnings rising, and the only way to "unlock" them is to deposit your own money, usually in cryptocurrency. The earnings on screen are not real.

## How common is it?

The FTC reports that losses to job scams reported in the US rose from $90 million in 2020 to $286 million in 2023. It estimates that about 20,000 people reported task scams in the first half of 2024, compared with about 5,000 in all of 2023. Most fraud goes unreported, so the real numbers are higher.

## What to do

- Ignore unexpected job texts. Real employers don't recruit that way.
- Never pay anyone to get a job or to get paid.
- Don't trust anyone who offers money for likes, ratings or reviews.
- Forward the text to 7726 (US, UK and Canada), then delete it. In Australia, report it to [Scamwatch](https://www.scamwatch.gov.au).
- In the US, report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).
