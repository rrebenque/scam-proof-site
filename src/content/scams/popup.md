The US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) says real security warnings and error messages never ask you to call a phone number. A pop-up that does is the scam itself, usually from a website, not a virus on your computer.

## How to close it

- Close the browser. If it won't close, on Windows press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open Task Manager, pick the browser and choose End task.
- If the screen is still stuck, restart your computer.
- When the browser opens again, don't restore the previous tabs.

## Why it works

The warning is designed to scare you into acting before you think. Once you call, the "technician" sounds helpful and patient, and remote access looks like a normal part of a repair.

## Already called or let them in?

- Turn off the computer's internet connection.
- Call your bank on the number on your card, and change your passwords from a different device.
- Have the computer checked by someone you trust.
- Go to [I've been scammed](/been-scammed/) to report it.
