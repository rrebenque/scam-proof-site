The US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) has published a real scam robocall that uses the exact line "suspicious incoming charge of $1,499." The FTC says these calls and texts are scams, and that you should check your account yourself in the official app or website.

## Why it works

A big charge is scary, and the call offers a quick fix. Once you're talking to a person, the "refund" sounds like help, and the caller ID may even show the company's name. The FTC says caller ID can be faked.

## What to do

- Hang up. Don't press any number, and don't call back the number that called you.
- Open the Amazon app or type the website address yourself. If there's no order, there's nothing to cancel.
- Never give your card number or remote access to someone who called you.
- Report the call at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov) in the US.

Already gave your card details? Call your card company now, using the number on the back of the card. Then see [I've been scammed](/been-scammed/).
