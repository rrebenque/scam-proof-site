The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) says it will not demand immediate payment by Interac e-transfer, Bitcoin, prepaid credit cards or gift cards, and will not threaten you with arrest or prison. A call that does any of these is a scam.

## What to do

- Hang up. You don't need to argue or explain.
- If you're worried you really owe something, sign in to your CRA My Account yourself, or call the CRA on a number from canada.ca.
- Report the call to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre, whether you paid or not. The RCMP says your report helps.

## Already paid?

- Call your bank now. If you paid with crypto, also contact the company that runs the machine you used.
- Report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre at 1-888-495-8501, and to your local police.
- See [I've been scammed](/been-scammed/) for the other steps.
