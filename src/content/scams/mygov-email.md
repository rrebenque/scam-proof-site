The ACMA says myGov will never send you an email or SMS with a link. myGov does send messages to say you have something new in your myGov Inbox, but they never include a link to sign in. Scammers copy those messages with lines like "You have a new message in your myGov inbox – click here to view."

If a scammer gets your sign-in details, they can sign in to your real myGov account and change your bank details, so refunds from the ATO or other linked services go to them.

## What to do

- Don't click the link, button or QR code, and don't reply.
- Sign in to myGov yourself, in the official myGov app or by typing my.gov.au into your browser.
- Email the scam to reportascam@servicesaustralia.gov.au, or report it at scamwatch.gov.au.

## Already entered your details?

- Sign in to myGov yourself and change your password, then check your contact and bank details.
- Call the Services Australia Scams and Identity Theft Helpdesk. Find the number on Services Australia's "Help if a scam or identity theft has affected you" page.
- If you gave bank details or sent money, call your bank now.
