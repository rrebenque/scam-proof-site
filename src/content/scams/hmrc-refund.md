HMRC says it will never send notifications of a tax rebate by text, or ask for personal or payment information that way. In the 12 months to 31 July 2025, HMRC received more than 170,000 scam referrals, and more than 47,000 were about fake tax refunds.

## What to do

- Don't tap the link or reply.
- Forward the text to HMRC on 60599, or free to 7726, then delete it.
- To check whether you're really owed money, sign in to your HMRC online account or the HMRC app yourself. HMRC says that's where to claim refunds.

## Already entered your card details?

- Call your bank now, using the number on the back of your card.
- Report it to Report Fraud on 0300 123 2040 (England, Wales and Northern Ireland), or Police Scotland on 101.
